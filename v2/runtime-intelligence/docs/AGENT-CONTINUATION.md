## Product law
The advanced Lit implementation is the gold-standard experience. Generic/framework expansion should preserve its depth while moving intelligence into framework-neutral core modules.

## Challenge every proposed addition on
- evidence quality;
- causal honesty;
- cross-framework leverage;
- runtime overhead;
- privacy;
- whether Chrome DevTools/Lighthouse already solves it adequately.

## Mission 02 challenge
Do not merely draw a graph. Every edge must carry its own evidence level and attribution quality. Temporal adjacency must remain correlation unless a framework/runtime source can prove a stronger relationship.
